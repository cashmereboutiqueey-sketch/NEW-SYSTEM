# Cashmere OS — Business Dictionary v1

| Term | Arabic | Definition |
|---|---|---|
| Factory | المصنع | Contract manufacturing entity that produces goods and charges the Brand at transfer price. |
| Brand | البراند | Cashmere Boutique retail/commercial entity. |
| Group | المجموعة | Consolidated view of Factory + Brand after intercompany elimination. |
| Style | موديل | Base garment design and primary costing level. |
| Variant | متغير | Combination of one colour and one size. |
| SKU | كود الصنف | Unique product code: STYLENAME-COLORCODE-SIZE. |
| BOM | قائمة الخامات | Materials and quantities required to make a style. |
| SMV | الزمن المعياري | Standard minutes required to perform the defined operations for a style. |
| Minute Rate | تكلفة الدقيقة | Factory conversion cost allocated to one productive minute. |
| Utilisation | الاستغلال | Share of available capacity that is actually booked/used. |
| Efficiency | الكفاءة | Earned standard minutes relative to clocked working minutes. |
| Idle Capacity Cost | الطاقة العاطلة | Difference between actual minute rate and full-capacity minute rate. |
| Transfer Price | سعر التحويل | Arm's-length internal selling price from Factory to Brand. |
| Cost Snapshot | لقطة التكلفة | Immutable frozen cost basis attached to a confirmed production order. |
| Landed Cost | التكلفة الفعلية الواصلـة | Purchase price plus freight and customs/clearing. |
| Inventory Lot | لوط مخزون | Traceable inventory quantity/value received from one source transaction. |
| FIFO | الوارد أولاً يصرف أولاً | Inventory valuation/consumption method based on oldest available lot first. |
| WIP | تحت التشغيل | Work-in-process inventory between raw material and finished goods. |
| Finished Goods | تام الصنع | Completed products ready for sale/transfer. |
| Dead Stock | المخزون الراكد | Stock exceeding the configured age/slow-moving threshold. |
| Contribution Margin | هامش المساهمة | Effective revenue minus Brand variable cost. |
| Break-even | نقطة التعادل | Units/revenue needed to cover the defined Brand fixed-cost pool. |
| CCC | دورة الكاش | Cash conversion cycle from raw material through production, finished goods and collection, net of supplier credit. |
| Capital Locked | رأس المال المحبوس | Cash currently tied up in inventory/receivables or other working capital. |
| Moderator Order | طلب المودريتور | Social-media order manually created by a human moderator. |
| Sales Channel | قناة البيع | Origin of a sale, e.g. Shopify, moderator, POS, bazaar or wholesale. |
| POS | نقطة البيع | Physical retail sales interface using the shared order/inventory engine. |
| MRP | تخطيط احتياجات المواد | Material planning based on demand, BOM, inventory and purchasing constraints. |
| Collection | مجموعة | Group of styles launched/planned together. |
| Cost Center | مركز تكلفة | Accounting/management dimension used to attribute costs. |
| Accrual | استحقاق | Recognition of a cost/revenue when economically incurred/earned rather than when cash moves. |
| AP | الموردون / الذمم الدائنة | Amounts owed to suppliers/vendors. |
| AR | العملاء / الذمم المدينة | Amounts owed by customers. |
| Profit After Marketing | الربح بعد التسويق | Profit remaining after product and operating economics plus attributable marketing spend. |
